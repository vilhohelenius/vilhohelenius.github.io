About

I graduated in May 2026 as a diplomi-insinööri — the Finnish M.Sc. (Tech) — in Data Analytics from the University of Turku, minoring in statistics, after a B.Sc. in Information Technology from the same university. Along the way I kept finding the same pattern in every course: I'd rather build the thing than just read about it, so the projects below span from graded coursework to apps I'm still actively shipping.

Outside of models and matrices, I've spent the last several years working the floor at a sports goods store, which turned into an unplanned second education in what actually makes someone come back and buy their next pair of shoes. That's also where the sports-analytics interest comes from: I've watched enough gait analyses and split times to want to build tools around them, not just read about them in a paper.

Most of what's below is self-directed — built because I wanted the tool to exist, not because a syllabus asked for it.

In progress

AI Coach — Training Dashboard

A manual-log training dashboard for runners, built because every wearable app I tried demanded more discipline logging than it gave back in return. Every morning I log training activity, sleep, and subjective feel, then the app calls the Claude API to turn that log into a plain-language readiness briefing instead of a wall of charts.

No OAuth, no watch sync, on purpose — the friction of writing it down by hand is part of the point. Lightweight support for cycling, swimming, disc golf, and hiking rides alongside running.

Shoe Fit Explorer

A tool that plots roughly 70 running shoe models on a map of width against midsole softness, so a runner can see where a shoe actually sits before buying it — built after years of watching spec sheets fail to explain how a shoe feels underfoot. A short intake questionnaire narrows the field, and a step‑softer / step‑stiffer navigator surfaces neighboring recommendations.

A companion to KinttuScan, the camera-based foot scanner on my CV — that one starts from the foot, this one starts from the shoe data. KinttuScan repository →

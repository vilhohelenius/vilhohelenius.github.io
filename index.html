    Morning Hockey

    Last night's NHL results, with a focus on the Finnish players: their goals, assists and saves in each game, plus standings, scoring leaders, a Finnish scoring race, playoff bracket and a page for every player and all 32 teams. Open it with your morning coffee and the box scores are already waiting.

    Logging in is deliberately light — a username is enough — and saves your favorite teams and players. A spoiler-hide setting keeps the scores out of sight until you're ready.

    Built with TypeScript, Cloudflare Pages and D1, D3 charts, the public NHL API. The interface is in Finnish.

    Sports analytics · October 2026

    xGoalBoost × TabPFN

    Can a tabular foundation model predict whether an NHL shot becomes a goal as well as tuned gradient boosting? I trained expected-goals models on about 365,000 shot attempts from three seasons of MoneyPuck data and put TabPFN-3.5 head to head with XGBoost and CatBoost — the same question as my thesis, on a hockey puck.

    On equal training data TabPFN came out ahead; with all the data, XGBoost closed the gap and stayed marginally better.

    Test AUC, trained on 2023–24, tested on 2025 shots
    ModelTraining shotsAUC

    The first model scored 0.84 AUC, better than MoneyPuck's own, which was too good to trust. The cause was a data leak: penalty-time columns are recorded after the shot, and a power-play goal ends the penalty. Removing them brought the score back to a believable 0.80.

    Built with Python, XGBoost, CatBoost, TabPFN-3.5 (Prior Labs API).

    Saunatonttu

    Heating a sauna costs less when electricity is cheap. Every day around noon, this checks the spot price for the evening hours (18–21 by default) and sends a push notification to my phone if any hour is under 10 cents per kWh. That's the signal to light the stove.

    Built with Python, GitHub Actions, ntfy, the free porssisahko.net price API.

    Web app

    AI Coach: training dashboard

    A manual-log training dashboard for runners, built because every wearable app I tried demanded more discipline logging than it gave back in return. Every morning I log training activity, sleep, and subjective feel, then the app calls the Claude API to turn that log into a plain-language readiness briefing instead of a wall of charts.

    No OAuth, no watch sync, on purpose — the friction of writing it down by hand is part of the point. Lightweight support for cycling, swimming, disc golf, and hiking rides alongside running.

    Built with Next.js 15, React 19, Prisma 6, SQLite, Recharts, Tailwind CSS, Claude API.

    Running shoes

    Shoe Fit Explorer

    A tool that plots roughly 70 running shoe models on a map of width against midsole softness, so a runner can see where a shoe actually sits before buying it — built after years of watching spec sheets fail to explain how a shoe feels underfoot. A short questionnaire narrows the field, and a step‑softer / step‑stiffer navigator surfaces neighboring recommendations.

    A companion to KinttuScan, the camera-based foot scanner on my CV — that one starts from the foot, this one starts from the shoe data.

    Built with Vite, React, DuckDB‑wasm. The interface is in Finnish.
